# GE Beauty — Booster upsell pairings, derived from the combo-repeat study

**Also in the live artifact:** `ge-beauty-retention-study` (Cowork) — this doc is the
durable spec; the artifact has the full sortable data behind these picks. Built from
`docs/booster-combo-repeat-study.md` / `gebeauty/growth/module-a/booster-combo-repeat.out.json`.

## The rule, in one line

**Any time a booster is in the cart/PDP/post-purchase upsell slot, offer Máscara
condicionadora (002) first, Shampoo sem sulfato (001) second — and never offer Máscara
Mayday (121) or Melon Mood travel-size (029).** That's not a per-booster guess; it's the
same two winners and the same two losers across all five boosters, independently.

## Why these two, specifically

| Partner | Appears in top-3 for | Sample size when paired |
|---|---|---|
| Máscara condicionadora (002) | **4 of 5 boosters** | Large every time (348–991) |
| Shampoo sem sulfato (001) | **4 of 5 boosters** | Large every time (366–1,073) |

No other partner shows up more than twice. These aren't just statistically strongest —
they're the two products with both high lift AND large combo samples, which is the
combination that actually matters for an upsell rule you'll run at volume.

## Hard avoid-list — confirmed across every booster tested

| Partner | Appears in bottom-2 for |
|---|---|
| Máscara Mayday (121) | **5 of 5 boosters** |
| Melon Mood travel-size (029) | **5 of 5 boosters** |

Unanimous. If either of these is currently surfaced as a companion/upsell item when a
booster is in the cart (checkout upsell app, PDP "frequently bought with," email
cross-sell), that's actively working against retention and should be swapped out.

## Per-booster pairing, ranked

| Booster | Solo repeat rate | #1 pairing | #2 pairing | Lift at #1 (vs. solo) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hidratante (020) | **6.1%** (weakest solo of all 5) | Shampoo sem sulfato — 21.1% (n=758) | Máscara condicionadora — 21.1% (n=754) | **+10.8pp** |
| Definição (021) | 11.3% | Máscara condicionadora — 20.7% (n=849) | Shampoo sem sulfato — 19.9% (n=942) | +8.3pp |
| Antioxidante (023) | 22.6% (n=102, thin) | Máscara condicionadora — 25.3% (n=348) | Shampoo sem sulfato — 22.9% (n=366) | +9.4pp |
| Antifrizz (022) | 12.7% | Máscara condicionadora — 21.3% (n=991) | Shampoo sem sulfato — 21.1% (n=1,073) | +6.8pp |
| Fortificante (019) | 9.6% | Travel máscara condicionadora — 23.6% (n=292) | Travel shampoo sem sulfato — 23.2% (n=285) | +6.7pp |

Fortificante is the one exception where a travel-size variant edges out the full-size —
worth testing both, since travel sizes are also a lower-friction, lower-price upsell ask.

## Priority call: hidratante first

Hidratante has the lowest solo repeat rate of any booster (6.1%) **and** the biggest
jump when paired (+10.8pp, to 21.1%) — the single highest-leverage fix available. If
upsell slots have to be rolled out one booster at a time, start here.

## Correction flag, not just an addition

Booster definição's largest existing combo in the data is **definição + full-size
leave-in proteção térmica** (n=2,440 — bigger than any of its winning pairings above).
That pairing sits **below** the catalogue baseline (13.6% vs. 14.1%). If today's
merchandising already nudges definição buyers toward the leave-in, that's the specific
thing to change — redirect that upsell slot to máscara condicionadora or shampoo sem
sulfato instead.

## Confidence notes

- Antioxidante's own solo baseline is thin (n=102) — its combo lift is directionally
  the strongest in the whole study (+11.2pp vs. catalogue baseline, the best absolute
  number of any pairing) but treat it as the least statistically certain of the five.
- All other boosters' top pairings sit on samples in the several-hundred-to-thousand
  range — solid.
- Data is the same 2026-07-22 dump used throughout this study (~2.5 weeks stale as of
  writing) — fine for directional merchandising decisions, re-pull before treating any
  single number as precise.

## Where this plugs in

Not scoped here (needs a decision on which surface to change first): PDP "frequently
bought together" module, cart-page upsell, post-purchase upsell app, or a WhatsApp/email
cross-sell nudge triggered off a booster-only order. The pairing logic above is
surface-agnostic — same two winners, same two avoids, wherever it gets implemented.
