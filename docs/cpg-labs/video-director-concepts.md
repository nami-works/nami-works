# GE Beauty — per-product video concepts (v2)

**Lane:** faceless + hair-out (production-method constraint) · hero ritual · hook → desire → offer · 8 to 15s for Meta Reels (9:16) · calm body with arrested-motion hook.

## Why hair-out and face-out

The brand at large uses faces and styled hair in its visual identity (Instagram, print mockups, lifestyle). For THIS skill specifically, faces and hair are out because AI-generated humans and hair carry misleading-claim risk on Meta. The brand-wide rule is unchanged; the AI-video skill's rule is stricter. Document this distinction so future contributors don't think GE Beauty avoids humans (it doesn't).

## The hook formula: arrested motion (not explosion)

Every hook opens with **composed stillness containing arrested motion** — a droplet *about to fall*, foam *just beginning* to bloom, a feather *suspended* mid-descent, mist *starting* to drift. The eye is hooked by the impending motion, not by an explosion. Within 1 to 1.5s, the motion completes calmly (the droplet lands, the foam blooms, the feather touches, the mist arrives). No camera shake, no jump cut, no chaos.

This is the Aesop signature: things HAPPEN, slowly, beautifully.

## Hair-proxy library

When a concept needs to demonstrate a hair claim WITHOUT showing hair:

| Hair concept | Proxy |
|---|---|
| Cascade / fall | A length of natural silk thread or satin ribbon in slow motion |
| Strand under tension | A single silk thread between two fingertips |
| Frizz vs smooth | Frayed fabric weave vs polished silk surface |
| Curl pattern | A coiled silk ribbon, a spiral of piped gel on marble |
| Hair gloss | Light reflection on a polished surface (porcelain, glass, brushed metal) |
| Brush glide | A wide-tooth comb passing through silk fibers |
| Heat protection | A cream-coated metal rod under a hot iron, droplets beading off |
| Humidity resistance | Water beads on a treated silk swatch vs absorbed on untreated |
| Volume / lift | A satin ribbon rising into a soft arch on an air puff |

## Audio direction (locked)

Four layers per concept:
1. **ASMR-grade sensorial sound** (foam crackle, cream pour, spray hiss, dropper click, droplet impact).
2. **Subtle ambient pad** (low-volume warm synth/piano bed under the body).
3. **Single musical sting on hook** (one chord/note at 0-2s).
4. **Sound-off readable** (2 captions only: hook caption + offer caption). Marcela voice, lowercase, coral on cream.

## Full-size hero formulas (9 concepts)

### 1. shampoo sem sulfato · R$95
*"Foam, undisturbed."*
- **Accent:** cream-only.
- **Hook (0-2s):** macro composed still on cream. A single drop of shampoo SUSPENDED mid-fall above a porcelain bowl. Light from upper-left. The drop completes its fall over 1.2s — touches the surface, dense white foam blooms outward in a perfect circle. Sting on impact. Caption coral: `limpeza profunda.`
- **Body (2-12s):** slow pour from the cream tube into the bowl. Foam climbs the rim in even tempo. A silk swatch lowers into the foam, lifts, lays softly on cream linen, visibly clean. Comb passes through the silk — no resistance.
- **Offer (12-15s):** tube reveal centered on cream. Caption coral lowercase: `sem sulfato.` then `no seu tempo, do seu jeito.` (tagline). 'ge' seal.

## House grammar (v2 — locked from brandbook)

### Hook formulas (use one per concept)

- **Arrested fall** — droplet, feather, cream rope, mist, spray puff suspended at the cusp of motion, then completes. Used for: shampoo, máscara, leave-in proteção, pluma, shampoo a seco, antifrizz, hidratante.
- **Suspended state demonstration** — silk swatch under UV, thread under tension, heat shimmer over silk, ribbon mid-coil. Used for: antioxidante, fortificante, primer liso, primer cachos, definição.
- **Object reveal** — slow self-acting reveal (jar lid twists open, lid lifts) without theatrical drama. Used for: mayday, melon mood.

### Body sequences

- Application via proxy (silk thread/ribbon/fabric/comb) on cream linen.
- Time-lapse for durability claims (5 min, 12h, 24h, 7 days).
- Dropper-into-product for boosters specifically.
- Side-by-side A/B for proof claims (UV fade, water bead-off, brush hair-count).

### Offer card formula

```
[product on cream]    [accent color band]

[benefit caption, coral lowercase]

   no seu tempo, do seu jeito.

       [ge ligature seal]
```

Optional second line under tagline for price/free shipping when DR-relevant.

### Brand seal (A/B test in production)

- **Variant A:** static 'ge' ligature on cream, ~0.5s hold.
- **Variant B:** small animated 'ge' formation/seal, ~0.7s.

Each campaign ships both, Meta picks the winner per audience.

4. **Then the skill build can begin** with confidence the IP is locked.
