Phase 0

Foundations

Done · 2026-05-12
What shipped
  • An eval set: 12 real routing decisions from late April through today, locked as the regression baseline the AI will be measured against
  • A geofence registry: barriers (Guanabara Bay), corridors (Botafogo→Tijuca, Botafogo→Niterói via the bridge), per-metro outlier thresholds, sparse-volume locations like Recife
  • The AI prompt v1: the spatial-reasoner's instructions and decision rules, version-tagged so we iterate without losing history
What changes for you
  • Nothing today — these are the artifacts the AI will read once Phase 1 ships
Phase 1

AI takes the wheel

Next · ~2 weeks out
What ships
  • When you run optimize, the system stops just clustering and starts reasoning about the routes
  • AI sees the map, knows about Guanabara Bay, knows about the Botafogo→Niterói corridor, knows what an outlier is
  • Proposes route splits with reasoning attached, not just optimizer math
  • Tags each proposed route with confidence and the rules it considered
What changes for you
  • Same morning routine — you still trigger optimize from nami_control.py
  • Fewer interruptions where we manually catch "wait, this should ride with Niterói" — the system catches what we catch together today
  • You review the AI's reasoning trail; it surfaces the call, you say yes or override
Example

Today we moved julia to the Niterói route because she sits on the bridge corridor. In Phase 1 that would have been the system's first proposal, with a one-line reasoning trail: "#80102 julia (Flamengo) is on the Botafogo→bridge corridor — naturally a drive-by stop on the way to Niterói. Moved from Rio route to Niterói route."

Phase 2

You stop showing up in the morning

~4 weeks out
What ships
  • The reasoning runs on its own via a 5-minute cron — no human trigger needed
  • Auto-cron picks up new orders, decides, dispatches, attaches reasoning, fires Lalamove
  • Starts on Shops Jardins first; expands to Rio and Recife once the SJ flow is stable
  • A daily dashboard surfaces what was dispatched, what reasoning was applied, anything flagged for review
What changes for you
  • The 10-15 minute morning routine disappears for SJ first, then Rio, then Recife
  • You check in on the dashboard once a day instead of driving the dispatch
  • You only intervene if something looks off — and the dashboard tells you when something does
Example

Tomorrow morning you wake up to find SJ's 3 new orders already dispatched in 2 clean routes. You don't have to do anything unless an alert pops up. Total time spent on Local Delivery: ~30 seconds glancing at the dashboard.

Phase 3

The system gets smarter from us

~6 weeks out
What ships
  • Post-mortem panel showing every AI decision from the last few days — what it chose, why, what it flagged
  • You mark wrong calls and explain why in plain text
  • The system extracts the rule, updates the geofence registry, applies the fix to future routes
  • A correction made once teaches the system forever — not just for the location it happened at, but for any location with the same shape
What changes for you
  • Your role shifts from "dispatcher" to "teacher"
  • Spend ~5 minutes a day reviewing yesterday's calls; flag anything wrong
  • Your judgment scales across every future route, every location, every operator who joins later
Example

If the AI keeps splitting Niterói + Copa on low-volume days when you'd prefer combined, mark those calls wrong with the reason: "only 2 orders today, R$20 saving worth the bridge crossing." The system updates the barrier-tolerance rule. From the next 2-order Rio day onward, the AI keeps them combined automatically.

How we'll know it's working

  • Coverage · 0% auto today → 100% auto by mid-July
  • Quality · AI matches operator-validated routing calls ≥90% of the time
  • Savings · Cost per delivered order in Q3 is ≥10% lower than today's baseline
  • Stability · Zero unplanned dispatch incidents requiring expedition-team triage in Q3
