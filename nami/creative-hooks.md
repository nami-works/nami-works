# NAMI Works — Creative Hook Backlog

Seed concepts for paid/organic creative. Each is an idea to develop, not finished copy or a produced asset. When one is ready to build, the flow is: lock the hook copy → hand to the creative-producer flow for the still-ad matrix.

Status legend: `idea` (captured, undeveloped) · `developing` (copy/variants in progress) · `ready` (approved for production) · `live`.

## HOOK-001 — Feed pattern interrupt, with something leaking through

**Status:** idea
**Registered:** 2026-07-30

**Concept.** An in-image simulation of the social feed itself — the ad renders *as* the surrounding feed UI (posts, chrome, familiar layout) so the eye reads it as native, then something breaks the plane and "leaks through" the fake feed. The leak is the payload: the NAMI message/mascot pushing through the mock interface.

**Why it could work.** Pattern interruption — the creative disguises itself as the environment, then violates it, which stops the scroll. The "leak through the feed" gives a literal visual metaphor for NAMI cutting through the noise / breaking out of the usual feed.

**Open questions to resolve before producing.**
- What exactly leaks through — the monkey mascot, a hand, product/UI of the AI ops system, a message?
- Which platform's feed do we simulate (Meta vs. TikTok chrome differ), and does mimicking native UI risk ad-policy issues?
- Static first, or is the leak inherently better as motion/video?

**Next step:** decide the "leak" element and target platform, then draft 2–3 hook lines.

## HOOK-002 — Monkey breaking tech in frustration

**Status:** idea
**Registered:** 2026-07-30

**Concept.** The NAMI monkey mascot losing it at a piece of technology — smashing / breaking something tech-related — out of frustration that its processes aren't working. Represents the *archaic* way of using AI (manual, brittle, fighting the tools) and not getting your way. The joke lands on the old approach; NAMI is the contrast/relief.

**Why it could work.** Uses the mascot's existing energy (the mark is already an open-mouthed, expressive monkey — see BRAND.md), which is where the brand personality is meant to live. Relatable frustration = shareable; sets up NAMI as the "there's a better way" answer.

**Open questions to resolve before producing.**
- What's the object being broken (keyboard, laptop, a spreadsheet on screen, a robot)? It should read as "old/archaic AI or manual ops," not just generic rage.
- How do we make the "this is the OLD way" framing unmistakable, so the frustration reads as the problem NAMI solves — not as NAMI itself being broken?
- Tone check against BRAND.md: the mark carries the personality, but the brand voice stays restrained. Keep the comedy in the visual, not in loud/hype copy.
- Still or short video? Frustration/destruction may want motion.

**Next step:** pick the object + the "old way" signifier, then draft hook lines that make NAMI the relief.
