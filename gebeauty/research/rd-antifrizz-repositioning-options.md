# Booster Antifrizz (GEB 022) — Repositioning Options

**Question:** the packaging must change (dropper fails on a viscous formula). That reopens the product's identity. Where should this formula live in the Brazilian market, and under what name/format/price, even if it leaves the "Boosters" line?
**Basis:** the formula's real behavior (viscous, oil-like, fragranced, multi-benefit leave-on finisher) + deep BR market research (category map, 21 verified competitor SKUs, format/price benchmark). Sources in the companion research notes.

---

## 1. The core reframe: this was never a booster

GE's other boosters are small, **unscented, 100% pure actives** meant to be **mixed into** other products. This one is the opposite: **viscous, fragranced, used neat as a finisher** ("controla o frizz e o volume, blinda da umidade, devolve brilho e maciez", plus proteção térmica 230°C). Customers already say it out loud: *"troquei meus óleos por ele"*, *"parece o leave-in dentro do vidrinho do booster"*.

In the Brazilian market, that exact benefit set (antifrizz + brilho + alinhamento + blindagem térmica/umidade, leave-on) is a well-defined shelf: **óleo finalizador / sérum antifrizz**. The closest real analogues:
- **Hidratei "Sérum Antifrizz Óleo Finalizador"** 60ml, pump, fragranced, **R$110** — literally fuses the three category words; proof the concept sells at 2x GE's price.
- **TRESemmé Blindagem Antifrizz Óleo Finalizador 5 em 1** — the mass template for GE's exact claim stack ("nutre, recupera brilho, alinha pontas, controla frizz", blindagem = humidity shield).
- **Braé Divine Óleo Capilar Ultraleve Antifrizz** 60ml, **R$130** — closest premium analogue; same "poucas gotas" finisher ritual, straddles óleo/sérum naming.
- **Widi Care Blindando a Juba** óleo 60ml, **~R$69** — closest on price and on the humidity angle ("Escudo Antiumidade"). This is the direct masstige threat.

**"Booster" is the single worst label for it:** it signals a mixing additive and files a hero finisher in a bargain-vial format.

---

## 2. Two decisions to make (independent axes)

**Axis A — Category / name frame** (kills the "booster" confusion):
- **Óleo finalizador** — truest to the viscous/oil texture and the "troquei meus óleos" testimonial; biggest, best-understood shelf. Risk: "óleo" triggers "vai pesar/engordurar?" so must foreground *ultraleve / não pesa / poucas gotas*.
- **Sérum (antifrizz / finalizador)** — reads more technical/premium, higher price headroom (R$90–120), and a "sérum" is understood as used-neat (not mixed). Slightly less texture-literal.
- **Hybrid "Sérum / Óleo Finalizador"** — the Hidratei/Braé move: captures both search terms and both cues. Recommended frame.
- **"Blindagem" as the claim spine** — regardless of category, "blinda da umidade / blindagem antifrizz" is a hot, high-recognition BR claim (TRESemmé, Novex, Widi, Acquaflora) that maps 1:1 to GE's humidity benefit. Own it in the name/hero copy.

**Axis B — Format + size + price** (the packaging change):
- **Format:** the dropper is the wrong dispenser *class* for a viscous formula. BR brands use **pump / válvula (airless)** for viscous oils/serums; **tube (bisnaga)** for creamy finishers. **Airless pump = primary recommendation** (handles viscosity, reads premium, protects a fragranced formula). Tube only if repositioned as a creamy finalizador.
- **Size/price reality:** 15ml at R$67 = **R$4.47/ml = prestige per-ml in a masstige aisle, defended only by a tiny size** = worst of both worlds. Category norm is **50–60ml at R$0.9–1.5/ml**. Widi Blindando a Juba gives 4x the volume for the same R$69 — GE loses the shopper-math fight at 15ml.
  - **B1 (recommended): size up** to 50–60ml, airless pump, **R$79–99**. Fixes value optics, keeps near-current unit economics at the top of masstige, fixes the dispenser.
  - **B2: stay small, go premium** — keep ~15–30ml but reframe explicitly as a **concentrated premium sérum** (must NOT read as a mixing vial); only works with prestige-grade branding/claims. Higher risk.

| **Format** | Airless pump | Airless pump or premium tube | Tube/bisnaga or mini-pump (drop the dropper) |

| **Effort** | Medium (package + size + line) | Medium (package + brand story) | Low (package only) |

---

## 4. Recommendation

**Option A**, with the hybrid claim spine: reposition as **"Óleo Finalizador Antifrizz"** (or Sérum/Óleo hybrid) in a **Finalizadores line**, **airless pump, 50–60ml, R$79–99**, hero claim **"blinda da umidade + antifrizz"**, supported by **quantified claims** (proteção térmica 230°C; brilho; "em poucas gotas, sem pesar") — the exact premium grammar Braé/Cadiveu/Widi use one tier below Kérastase. Keep the **fragrance as a named sensory hook** (Moroccanoil sells partly on its signature scent; GE's scent is already a review strength).

This single move fixes all three root problems at once: the **name** (no more "booster/mixing" confusion), the **format** (pump handles the viscosity), and the **value optics** (50–60ml stops losing the volume-for-money comparison), while staying in masstige unit economics.

If the appetite is lower, **Option C** is the safe interim (fix the dispenser now, decide the reposition later) — but it leaves the value and naming problems unsolved and the product will keep bleeding 1★ on "vem pouco / caro".

---

## 5. Open decisions for Lucas + R&D
1. **Category frame:** óleo vs sérum vs hybrid (Axis A). Affects name, price ceiling, and objection to pre-empt.
2. **Size:** size up to 50–60ml (recommended) vs stay concentrated/premium. Drives fill cost, price, and shelf comparison.
3. **Line placement:** create/assign a "Finalizadores" line vs keep under Boosters. Affects site nav, collections, cross-sell.
4. **Fragrance:** keep and feature (recommended) vs the unscented-booster convention. This product is the exception; it's already scented and loved for it.
5. **Claim substantiation:** confirm the 230°C thermal and humidity-shield claims are testable/held before they go on a repositioned pack (R&D + regulatory).
