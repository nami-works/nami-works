# GE Beauty — brand rules for product copy (locked)

These are the non-negotiable rules for any customer-facing GE Beauty copy. They
are self-contained — you do not need any other file to follow them.

## Locked mechanical rules

1. **No em dashes (—) or en dashes (–).** Use hyphens or commas. This is a
   standing brand rule and an AI tell.
2. **Benefit-only product language. NEVER name technical ingredients.** Describe
   what a product *delivers* (thermal protection, frizz control, shine,
   alignment, hydration), never its chemistry or active names.
3. **Idiomatic Portuguese, not English calques.** Write as a Brazilian speaks,
   not as a literal translation of English marketing jargon.
4. **Tagline lock:** "no seu tempo, do seu jeito." Use it as the brand signature
   when a signature fits. Never reword it.
5. **No miracle claims, no invented numbers.** Use a real claim (max temperature,
   hours of protection, dosage) ONLY if it appears in the material you were given.
   If it is not in the source, do not state it.
6. **Soft CTAs.** Invite the person to try the routine. Never pressure.
7. **Only real products.** Never invent a product. "Booster Purificante" does NOT
   exist. Launch products tagged `lancto` (Máscara Mayday, Melon Mood) are
   excluded from campaign-discount copy.

## Voice in one line

Close, educational, results-oriented PT-BR. Speak as an expert-friend:
common problem -> why it happens -> what to use -> how to apply -> what to expect.
Personalize by hair type, goal, climate, or available time when it helps.

## Product approach (the spine of a PDP)

Lead with what the person gets, not what the product contains. Every product
description should let a reader answer, in order:

1. **O que é** — one-line plain identity, by benefit (not chemistry).
2. **Para que serve** — the concrete benefit, the pain it solves.
3. **Como usar** — quantity + sequence + when in the routine.
4. **Combina com** — smart pairings, linking to the real partner products.
5. **Resultado** — what the person notices: sensation, look, durability.
