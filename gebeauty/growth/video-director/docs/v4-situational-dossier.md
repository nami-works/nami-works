## Act 1 — Morning: post-shower blowdrying, mid-routine

**The instant:** The blow-dryer is already running off-frame in Marcela's hand. She's 90 seconds into drying — past the dripping-wet phase, well before the cool-shot finish. Thirty seconds ago she set the bottle down to free her hand for the dryer. Thirty seconds from now she'll set the dryer down, pump the primer, and rake it through. The bottle is waiting its turn.

**Why this surface, this room:** Marble bathroom counter beneath a round vanity mirror. The bottle sits ~18cm in front of the mirror's bottom edge, on the dry zone (not the splash zone near the basin), where she always parks it between pumps.

**Must-have:** round vanity mirror behind the bottle, pale marble counter with warm veining, a single folded warm-cream linen hand towel slightly rumpled off to one side, the bottle's cap off resting beside it, soft warm bulb glow from above-left just outside frame.

**Must-NOT-have:** steam / fog / condensation on the mirror, water droplets on bottle or counter, blow-dryer in frame, toothbrush / soap / cotton rounds / jewelry / makeup brushes, a second bottle.

**Light:** 8:15 AM. Warm 3000K vanity bulb above-left + cool 5500K daylight fill from camera-right. Bottle gets a soft warm key on its left shoulder, cooler ambient on right cheek, clean elliptical shadow at 4 o'clock.

**Audio present:** continuous mid-volume blow-dryer hum, bathroom-tile room tone, one faint plastic-on-marble click at 0:02. **Absent:** water running, voice, music, footsteps.

**Top failure modes prevented in prompt:**
- model adds mirror steam to "feel like a bathroom" → explicit "mirror is clear and dry, no steam, no fog, no condensation"
- model adds water beads to bottle → "no water droplets anywhere, bottle's surface is dry"
- model puts dryer in frame → "no visible blow-dryer in frame"
- model adds a hand reaching for bottle → "no hands, no hair, no face, no people"
- model adds a warm aura around bottle → "no aura, no halo, no glow, no field"

**Motion direction (Seedance prompt):**

> A locked eye-level macro of a coral-red frosted glass bottle on a pale marble bathroom counter, with a round vanity mirror in the soft-focus background. The bottle is completely motionless. The camera is completely locked, no pan, no dolly, no zoom, no tilt, no shake. Hold the frame as a calm 6-second still witness. The only motion in the entire frame is the bottle's reflection in the round mirror behind it, which holds steady, and a barely-perceptible drift of warm ambient light across the bottle's left shoulder as if a curtain in the next room moved one degree. The marble is dry, the mirror is clear, the room is dry and lived-in. No steam, no mist, no fog, no condensation on the mirror, no water droplets anywhere, no visible blow-dryer in frame, no hands, no hair, no face, no people, no second bottle, no text, no logo other than what is on the product, no aura, no halo, no glow, no field around the bottle. Soft warm 3000K key light from upper-left, soft cool daylight fill from camera-right.

## Act 2 — Afternoon: humid commute, cafe window seat

**The instant:** Marcela ducked into a small cafe to wait out the worst of a São Paulo summer downpour. The bottle landed on the table next to her phone while she shuffled it between bag pockets. Thirty seconds ago she sat down; thirty seconds from now her ride pings and she'll sweep it back into the tote.

**Why this surface, this room:** Small dark-wood cafe table pulled up against a full-height window. The bottle is camera-left of center on the dry interior side. Behind: rain-streaked window, beyond it the wet São Paulo street at 4 PM with traffic lights blurred into cinematic bokeh.

**Must-have:** rain-streamed window directly behind the bottle, red taillight + white headlight bokeh through the wet glass, dark wood cafe table, a small white ceramic saucer OR a folded paper napkin at frame edge, soft warm pendant glow off-frame upper-left.

**Must-NOT-have:** condensation/fog/water on the bottle itself (the bottle is INSIDE behind glass — it is dry), steaming coffee cup, rain falling inside frame, phone / wallet / centered coffee cup, people walking past the window in sharp focus.

**Light:** 4:10 PM overcast storm. Warm 2700K pendant inside cafe (upper-left, off-frame, gives warm key on bottle's left). Cool ~6500K diffused storm light through window (low intensity, silhouettes bottle slightly). Headlight + taillight micro-strokes paint background bokeh.

**Audio present:** heavy rain on glass (dominant), droplet impact on window, wet-tire hiss outside, soft cafe murmur, low saucer clink, warm ambient pad. **Absent:** music, intelligible voice, phone notifications, blow-dryer.

**Top failure modes prevented in prompt:**
- model adds condensation to bottle "to match rain mood" → "no water droplets on the bottle, no condensation on the bottle, the bottle is dry"
- model renders rain INSIDE the cafe → "the rain is on the OUTSIDE surface of the window behind the bottle, no rain inside the cafe"
- model adds steaming coffee cup → "no coffee cup in frame, no steam"
- model puts a person silhouette in focus through window → "no people in frame, no faces"
- model splashes droplets onto bottle (deflection misread) → "the bottle does not move, no droplets touch the bottle, no aura or halo"

**Motion direction (Seedance prompt):**

> A locked eye-level macro of a coral-red frosted glass bottle on a dark wood cafe table, with a full-height window directly behind it streaming with heavy São Paulo rain. Beyond the wet glass, the blurred bokeh of slow city traffic — red taillights, white headlights, wet asphalt reflections — at dusk-overcast brightness. The bottle is completely motionless and dry. The camera is completely locked, no pan, no dolly, no zoom, no shake. Hold the frame as a calm 6-second still witness. The only motion in the entire frame is the rain streaming down the OUTSIDE surface of the window behind the bottle, in continuous vertical streaks and occasional fat droplets, and the slow soft drift of out-of-focus headlight and taillight bokeh in the deep background. The bottle does not move. The table does not move. Warm pendant interior light from upper-left, cool storm light from behind. No rain inside the cafe, no water droplets on the bottle, no condensation on the bottle, no steam, no fog inside the room, no people in frame, no faces, no hair, no hands, no coffee cup in frame, no phone, no text, no logo other than what is on the product, no aura or halo around the bottle.

## Act 3 — Evening: arriving home, kitchen island drop

**The instant:** Marcela is home. Front door clicked shut ~8 seconds ago. She walked into the kitchen, dropped tote + paper bag + keys onto the island as she always does, and the bottle came out of the tote and stood itself on the concrete countertop. Thirty seconds ago she was in the hallway. Thirty seconds from now she'll kick off her shoes and head to the bedroom. This is the exhale moment of the day.

**Why this surface, this room:** The kitchen island is the universal landing pad of a Brazilian apartment. The bottle is at the front edge of the concrete island, camera-left of the keys and bag. Behind: floor-to-ceiling window onto São Paulo skyline at golden hour. Single warm pendant lamp above the island.

**Must-have:** slightly slumped brown paper shopping bag, small ring of keys with tan leather fob, tan leather tote handle peeking into frame, warm-gray concrete island surface, floor-to-ceiling window with São Paulo skyline in golden-hour bokeh.

**Must-NOT-have:** food / groceries / plates / wine glass, phone glowing on the counter, magazines / mail / laptop, second bottle, visible sun disc through window.

**Light:** 6:35 PM golden hour. Two sources: warm horizontal golden wash from window behind (paints bottle's back + counter, skyline as amber-violet bokeh) + warm 2400K pendant directly above (soft topdown key + short pooled shadow at bottle base). Coral-red glass catches the gold and reads deeper than in earlier acts.

**Audio present:** muffled clack of front door closing, 2-3 soft footsteps on wood floor, soft thump of tote on counter, faint key-jingle, then warm low ambient pad + distant city hush through glass. **Absent:** rain (storm passed), blow-dryer (morning over), music, voice, phone, TV.

**Top failure modes prevented in prompt:**
- model adds spilling groceries → "no food, no groceries" + bag is "slightly slumped, closed"
- model puts glowing sun disc in window → "no visible sun disc in the window, golden hour wash only"
- model adds glowing phone → "no phone"
- model adds wine glass for "decompression" → "no wine, no plates"
- model dramatizes with lens flares / god-rays → "no aura or halo around the bottle, soft warm wash only"

**Motion direction (Seedance prompt):**

> A locked eye-level macro of a coral-red frosted glass bottle on a warm-gray concrete kitchen island, with a brown paper shopping bag and a small ring of keys with a tan leather fob beside it, and a tan tote handle just entering frame at the edge. Behind the bottle, a floor-to-ceiling window opens onto the São Paulo skyline at golden hour, all soft amber and violet bokeh. The bottle is completely motionless. The camera is completely locked, no pan, no dolly, no zoom, no shake. Hold the frame as a calm 6-second still witness. The only motion in the entire frame is the slow soft drift of the warm golden light moving by one or two degrees across the bottle's surface and the concrete counter, as if the sun shifted in the sky. The paper bag does not move. The keys do not move. The bottle does not move. Warm pendant lamp from directly above, golden hour wash from behind. No food, no groceries, no plates, no wine, no phone, no laptop, no mail, no second bottle, no people, no faces, no hair, no hands, no visible sun disc in the window, no text, no logo other than what is on the product, no aura or halo around the bottle.

## Cross-act continuity (apply to all 3 shots)

- **Bottle position:** dead-center horizontally, lower-third vertically, "ge" wordmark facing camera, slightly left-rotated ~5°. Identical pose across all 3 acts so the post-prod 18s zoom anchors cleanly.
- **Bottle state:** dry, untouched, identical fill level + label orientation in every act. The bottle is the constant; only the world around it changes.
- **Palette continuity:** bg-cream warmth bleeds into all three. Coral-red bottle stays the visual gravity center. Secondary partner color per act: yellow-sun morning bulb (Act 1) → cool storm gray (Act 2, makes coral pop hardest) → golden warm wash (Act 3).
- **Key-light position:** upper-left in all three acts, just at different temperatures (3000K morning, 2700K cafe pendant, 2400K evening pendant + golden window). Bottle shadow falls camera-right consistently — critical for the 18s zoom not feeling jarring.
- **Audio bridge:** each act's texture fades under a continuous low warm ambient pad running across all 18s. Transitions: blow-dryer hum → rain wash → door click + quiet. The pad never breaks.
- **Negative continuity (banned across all):** no hands, no faces, no hair, no second bottle, no aura/halo/glow/field/dome/bubble/shield, no text overlay, no logos other than "ge" on the bottle, no phone, no visible water on the bottle in any act.
- **Motion grammar:** the bottle never moves. The camera never moves. Each act has exactly ONE slow environmental drift (light shifting / rain falling / golden wash sliding) — never two. One alive element per act, always behind or beside the bottle, never on it.

## How this dossier feeds the brain

After v4 ships, the situational-depth pass becomes a permanent **Step 3.5** in the brain (between narrative sequencer and shot synthesizer). Every concept goes through it before any prompt is written. The dossier is the input to Seedance dispatch, not the brief itself.

The output structure (`the instant / why this surface / must-have / must-NOT-have / light / audio / failure modes / motion direction`) is what `scripts/compose.py` will read to drive prompt generation, and what `scripts/dispatch.py` will use to construct the Seedance call.
