03The economics

Solo mist can't clear the floor. The basket already does.

Sold alone at R$129, the mist has no room against a realistic ~R$49 acquisition cost — the 10% net floor caps CAC below what a cold buyer costs. 05Risks & what could kill it

R1
Cold traffic may basket less than the blended mist buyer.

The 56% basket rate includes warm and returning shoppers. Mitigation: the R$300/day test measures the paid cohort's own AOV directly — if cold buyers don't basket up, the day-7 gate kills it cheaply.

R2
Incrementality is unproven; platform numbers overstate lift.

Attribution is Meta-side. Mitigation: a Conversion Lift is owed before any real ramp; the floor here is judged on pre-media contribution, which is sound.

R3
Freight economics are mid-revision (Sélia → Unilog).

The cutover shaves ~R$7/order. Mitigation: every bundle absorbs it comfortably; only solo mist is sensitive — and solo is already off the table.
