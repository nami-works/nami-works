The approach — for your approval

Three lenses on the same questions.

Lens 1 · History
Priors + validation
Free, high-n, every product answerable today — including slow ones. Sets the starting beliefs and validates findings. Never used for causal claims (no holdout).
Lens 2 · Experiment
Causal proof
The holdout is the only thing that proves cause. One variable per wave, power-gated so cells never go thin. Confirms what history can only suggest.
Lens 3 · Induced-forward
The machine self-instruments
Every conversion logs SKUs, prior ownership, days-since, and a replenish/discovery flag — so cadence and adjacency maps sharpen automatically, wave over wave.
Now · replenishment priors from history→ Next wave · expiry-window test (1 variable)→ cadence archetypes as targeting rules→ discovery-framing test→ per-product measurement once pooled
Approve this method, and every wave sharpens the next — without gambling the experiment on thin cells.
History does the wide, cheap learning; the holdout spends its scarce power only on what it alone can settle; the machine accumulates the rest as it runs. That's the retention lab.
