Intellectual honesty · What we can't claim yet

Two things this round did not prove.

Refill wave (45–59d) unreadable

Shows −8.25pp, but that's 2 lucky buyers in a 19-person control — pure small-sample noise, not evidence the credit hurt. n=19 can't measure anything. Verdict: ignore until pooled across waves.

Sale-day A/B incomplete

Treatment fired (1,142 contacts, 2.63% repurchase) but the control arm never sent, so there's no comparison. We can't yet conclude anything about WhatsApp sale-day timing. Re-run with both arms.

One open operational item: the expiry extension stopped at 13,001 of ~14,111 holders. If we're now past 13/07, that window has closed and the ~1,110 remainder is likely moot — I'll confirm whether any still hold a live balance before we call it done.
