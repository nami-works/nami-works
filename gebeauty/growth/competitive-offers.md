# Competitive acquisition offers — teardown for the R$95 wash-routine bundle

> **Status: research synthesis. Nothing published, read-only.** Competitive-intelligence pull for the
> locked wash-routine offer (leve shampoo 001 + máscara 002 por R$95 + frete grátis, terceiro item de
> presente à escolha: leave-in travel 011 R$47 ou shampoo a seco 008 R$69).
> Source: Foreplay ad index (live ads, longest-running first) + public web for Cafellow.
> Pulled 2026-07-23 by content/hook lead.
>
> **"Working" signal used:** longevity (days running) + creative velocity (many live variants of the
> same offer). Both together = the offer is paying back and the brand keeps feeding it.

## 1. The three brands Lucas flagged

### Caffeine Army (parent) / SuperCoffee (product) — BR DTC, food/drink
The most instructive of the three: they run a **full acquisition-offer stack**, not one promo. Every
mechanic below is live and high-velocity right now.

| Offer mechanic | Hook / angle | Why it works | Ad |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free-sample tripwire.** 5 sachês grátis, você paga só o frete, e recebe um voucher no mesmo valor do frete de volta. | "SuperCoffee Grátis???? Por tempo limitado, receba 5 sachês GRÁTIS. Pague apenas o frete." | Running **~105 days** with many variants. The voucher-back move makes the frete feel refunded, so the perceived price is truly zero, pure trial with the risk removed. | [Free-sample tripwire](https://app.foreplay.co/discovery?ad=XpGDJj2MQCj8jwAd6eSQ) |
| **Tripwire, price-anchored reframe.** Same offer, newer creative: "5 amostras por R$0,00, pague R$20,90 de frete." | "A vaga é sua. 5 amostras de SuperCoffee por R$0,00." | They A/B the *framing* of the identical mechanic (grátis + voucher vs. "R$0,00 + frete explícito"). Worth copying the discipline: same offer, two money-framings. | [R$0,00 + frete framing](https://app.foreplay.co/discovery?ad=f2Xy5JwxqByDBgOvFvDO) |
| **Tiered spend GWP (compre e ganhe por faixa).** Frete grátis; multipack grátis acima de R$349,90; multipack + necessaire + 5 sachês acima de R$489,90. | "Sabor e energia em uma dose. Frete grátis. 🎁 Multipack acima de R$349,90." | ~38 days. Gifts escalate with basket size, pushes AOV up without a % cut. The gift is the headline, not the discount. | [Tiered GWP](https://app.foreplay.co/discovery?ad=AdF6t5wDqZMf88SXOYI1) |
| **Subscription with stacked brindes.** "Economize R$500 em 12 meses + brinde na 1ª caixa: 1 lata = caneca; 2+ latas = caneca + mixer." | "Assine Prime e economize mais de R$500 no seu SuperCoffee." | **~104 days.** The gift scales with commitment (1 vs 2 units), and the savings are framed as an absolute R$ number, not a %. | [Subscription brindes](https://app.foreplay.co/discovery?ad=rXB6SlrmGypGrCaC3KIp) |

**Caffeine Army takeaway for us:** their whole funnel is *value-add, gift-led, absolute-R$-framed*, almost
never "X% OFF" in the hook. That is exactly the register our copy-drafts already chose. The free-sample
tripwire is the one mechanic we do NOT run and probably should test downstream (see section 3).

### Cafellow — BR DTC, coffee in sachês (not in Foreplay's index; pulled from public web)
Cafellow does not surface in the Foreplay ad index (no tracked Meta page on file), so this is from their
live site, not ad-library velocity. Their **acquisition lever is the subscription**, framed on value-add:

- **"ECONOMIA TODO MÊS + FRETE GRÁTIS"** is the primary hook. Savings framed as a monthly habit, not a sale.
- Tiered subscription: 20% / 22% / 24% off by tier, **free shipping**, **monthly surprise gift (brinde surpresa)**,
  a **free box of ground coffee every 3 active months**, and early access to limited editions.
- Per-sachet price is spelled out ("R$3,75 por sachê") so a premium box reads as an affordable daily ritual.
- Emotional hook alongside the value: "Quanto você pagaria por um café que não te dá ansiedade?"
- Product line is characters ("Fellows": Determinado, Sensual, Criativo), which makes "monte seu kit" feel
  like a personality/choice exercise, not a discount grab.

**Cafellow takeaway for us:** the per-unit reframe ("R$3,75 por sachê") and "economia como hábito" are the
premium-safe way to make a low ticket feel like a smart ritual, which is precisely the tension in our brief
(R$95 must read as "começar uma rotina", not liquidação). Their gift is a *surprise* and a *recurring*
freebie, both loyalty devices rather than urgency devices.

Sources: [Cafellow site](https://cafellow.com.br/), [Cafellow assinatura](https://assinatura.cafellow.com.br/), [Cafellow kits](https://cafellow.com.br/collections/kits), [Nica por aí — Cafellow sachê](https://www.nicaporai.com/2025/04/cafellow-1-marca-de-cafe-em-sache-do.html).

### Recurring winning structures (the pattern across all of the above)
1. **Gift-led, not discount-led.** The winners say "ganhe / o terceiro é por nossa conta / brinde", almost
   never "X% OFF" in the hook. Discount, when present, is a secondary code, not the headline.
2. **The free item seeds the recurring habit.** Hidratei gifts shampoo + conditioner (the wash routine);
   Caffeine Army gifts samples then converts to subscription. The gift is chosen to drive recompra, exactly
   our spine.
3. **Dedicated offer landing page.** B.O.B and Caffeine Army both send ads to a purpose-built offer URL, not
   a generic PDP. Our lp-offer-mockup is already correct here.
4. **High creative velocity on one stable offer.** The winners are not many offers, they are one offer with
   dozens of creative variants running for months. Longevity beats novelty.
5. **Absolute-R$ or per-unit value framing.** "Economize R$500", "R$3,75 por sachê", "vale R$47 grátis".
   Numbers that feel like value gained, not price cut.
6. **Free shipping is table stakes**, always named in the hook.
7. **BOGO / leve-3-pague-2 is the dominant BR beauty mechanic**, always dressed as a gift ("o terceiro é por
   nossa conta") to protect positioning.
8. **Tripwire (free/near-free sample, pay shipping)** is the aggressive top-of-funnel play (Caffeine Army),
   distinct from the bundle play, and worth isolating as its own cohort if we test it.

## 3. Transferable recommendations for OUR campaign

Our locked offer already sits inside the winning pattern (gift-led, routine-seeding, dedicated LP, value-add
over discount). The tweaks below sharpen the framing and add tested elements, without touching price.

### Offer-framing tweaks
- **Lead with "o terceiro é por nossa conta", verbatim.** Hidratei runs this exact phrase on a 271-day offer.
  It is the single most proven premium-safe way to say "free third item" in BR beauty. Our current hero says
  "o terceiro é o nosso presente", which is on-strategy; "por nossa conta" is the battle-tested variant to A/B.
- **Add a per-unit / per-item value cue the way Cafellow does.** Alongside "vale R$47 / R$69 grátis", a line
  like "a dupla que limpa e nutre sai por R$47 cada" reframes R$95 as a smart per-item price, reinforcing
  "começar uma rotina" over "saldo". Keep it optional in the hero, exact after gift choice.
- **Keep anchoring the riscado on the pair (R$190), never on the sum of three.** Confirmed correct: every
  winner anchors value on the desirable items and treats the extra as gift, never discounts the whole basket.
- **Name the routine as the reason, like Hidratei seeds shampoo + conditioner.** Our "shampoo + máscara +
  shampoo a seco = a rotina que vira hábito" is the same move. Make the routine logic explicit in one line so
  the gift reads as useful, not as clearance.

### Hook angles to test (PT-BR, GE voice: value-add / premium, no em dash, ingredient-as-proof)
Add these to the three angles already in copy-drafts:

- **Gift-verbatim (Hidratei-proven):** "leve shampoo + máscara, o terceiro é por nossa conta."
- **Routine-seed:** "a rotina de lavagem completa: limpa, nutre e ainda ganha o terceiro."
- **Per-item reframe:** "dois best-sellers por R$47 cada, e o terceiro é presente."
- **Ingredient-as-proof, gift-led:** "leave-in que protege até 230°C ou shampoo a seco com biotina, você
  escolhe o presente." (only the gift items carry verifiable claims; shampoo + máscara stay at benefit level
  per our locked rules)
- **Choice-as-autonomy (premium, anti-scarcity):** "você escolhe o presente que combina com o seu momento."
- **Trial-of-the-routine (loyalty framing):** "experimente a rotina inteira e veja por que quem começa, volta."

### Elements worth adding (urgency / social proof / guarantee)
- **Social proof in the hook, Hidratei-style.** Pull the real Loox rating + review count for shampoo 001 and
  máscara 002 (both best-sellers) into the hero chips. Do not invent, pull live. The winners lean on
  "mais vendidos" and creator endorsements heavily.
- **Creator-coupon / UGC lane (Hidratei's engine).** A named-coupon creator layer turns discount into personal
  endorsement and gives clean attribution. Fits our value-add rule because the creator frames it as a routine
  they use, not a price cut. Flag for /crm-director + affiliates.
- **Soft, real urgency only.** BR winners use "por tempo limitado" and "estoques limitados", but our brief
  bars scarcity language for positioning reasons. Safer GE-voice substitute: a gift-availability frame
  ("presente incluso enquanto durar a ação") only if operationally true. Prefer the autonomy frame over urgency.
- **Comment-to-engage (Vhita, 124 days).** For a top-of-funnel prospecting ad, a "comente ROTINA" trigger can
  lift engagement and open a DM/remarketing lane. Test as one prospecting variant, not the core.
- **Guarantee.** None of the BR winners lead with a money-back guarantee; the gift itself is the risk-reversal.
  Our free third item + free shipping already carries that load. No need to add a guarantee line.

### Downstream test worth isolating (not part of this offer)
- **Free/near-free sample tripwire (Caffeine Army's 105-day play).** A "pague só o frete" mini-size tripwire
  is the most aggressive acquisition mechanic in this set and a different beast from the bundle. If tested, run
  it as its own segregated cohort judged on downstream payback (per the CGO acquisition-cohort guardrail), not
  blended into the R$95 bundle KPIs.

### What to avoid
- **No "% OFF", "desconto", "liquidação", "queima", "última chance" in the hook.** Confirmed by the winners:
  the strongest long-running offers lead with the gift and absolute value, not the cut. Our copy-drafts already
  hold this line, keep it.
- **Do not anchor the riscado on the three-item sum.** Reads as fire sale (already flagged in copy-drafts).
- **Do not out-discount ManClub / stack coupons on top of the gift.** Our lever is the gift + free shipping;
  adding a % code on top would signal clearance and erode the premium read.
- **Do not borrow scarcity theatrics** ("estoques super limitados", B.O.B) that fight our positioning. Use
  autonomy ("você escolhe") as the pull instead.
