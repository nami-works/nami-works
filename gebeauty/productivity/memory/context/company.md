# Company context — GE Beauty / Grupo GE

## What it is

GE Beauty (gebeauty.com.br) is a Brazilian haircare/beauty brand. It sits within "Grupo GE" and is tied to Garotas Estúpidas, the beauty-media brand associated with Camila Coutinho. Sold direct-to-consumer (Shopify) and through retail/distribution partners and delivery channels (Rappi).

## Tools & systems

- **Shopify** — storefront and orders.
- **Omie** — ERP (fiscal, financial, cadastros).
- **Monday.com** — work/project management (account: garotas-estupidas; workspace "[NOVO] Grupo GE"). ~15 active members.
- **Emplifi**, **Amazon DSP** — marketing tooling / ads.
- Custom internal tooling / integrations built on Shopify + Omie (CPG Labs).
