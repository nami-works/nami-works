# Glossary — GE Beauty / Grupo GE

Decoder ring for shorthand, acronyms, people, partners, and codenames used in Lucas's tasks.

## Acronyms & terms

- **ICMS** — Brazilian state VAT-style sales tax. Rates/rules vary by state (MA, RN, PI seen).
- **DIFAL** — Diferencial de Alíquota; interstate ICMS differential.
- **PJ** — pessoa jurídica; contractor engaged as a company (vs. employee).
- **CLT** — Consolidação das Leis do Trabalho; a registered employee.
- **G&G** — Gente & Gestão; People & Management / HR function.
- **R&D** — product research & development (formulas, packaging, formats).
- **PMO** — project management office (board "PMO GE Beauty" exists).
- **seeding** — sending product to influencers/creators for organic content.
- **GEB01** — internal entity/batch code (task: "Encerramento GEB01").
- **dissídio** — annual collective wage adjustment (Brazilian labor).
- **giro** — inventory turnover / sell-through.
