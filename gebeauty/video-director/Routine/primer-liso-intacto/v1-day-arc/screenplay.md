# Screenplay — Primer Liso Intacto day-arc with Marcela

**Throughline:** The 24-hour humidity shield holds. Marcela applies the primer in the morning; the storm tests it at midday; her hair survives the day; the bottle is the silent protection she trusts.

Grounded in GE Beauty's actual usage docs:
- `ai_readiness.complete_description`: "Primer finalizador… proteção térmica até 230°C e blindagem contra a umidade por até 24 horas. Realinha temporariamente os fios, criando efeito guarda-chuva que impede a absorção de umidade do ambiente."
- `passo_a_passo`: (1) Aplique nos cabelos úmidos antes de secar ou modelar. (2) Seque com secador e finalize como preferir. (3) Reaplique pequenas quantidades nos dias seguintes para manter o alinhamento.
- `combinations`: Apply on damp hair BEFORE the heat tool. Substitutes leave-in at the finishing step.

São Paulo. Summer heat. A storm is coming in by mid-afternoon.

---

## Scene 1 — 7:50 AM. Bathroom. (The application moment, just past.)

Marcela just stepped out of the shower. Hair damp. She put on her cream silk robe, walked to her vanity, and applied primer liso intacto exactly per the brand's instruction: damp hair, length to ends, before any heat. She set the bottle down on the counter. Then she picked up her brushed-metal salon-grade dryer and worked through her hair — methodical, two minutes of warm air shaping the wave at her crown. Her morning ritual. When her hair is dry and the umbrella effect is fully sealed, she clicks the dryer off. The room goes suddenly quiet. She walks the dryer to the counter where the bottle still stands, and sets it down beside it — the morning's tools, both retired.

**Frame:** Bottle alone on the cream marble counter. 0–3.6s the dryer runs off-frame at full power (she's sealing the umbrella). At 3.4s the audio cuts (she switched it off). Then her cream silk robe-sleeved hand sweeps in from the right carrying the dryer and sets it down beside the bottle. By 4.8s the hand is gone; the dryer sits in position. **The bottle is never touched** (she's done with it for now). The implicit promise: her hair is now sealed for 24 hours.

---

## Scene 2 — 3:15 PM. Cafe on Av. Paulista. (Hour 7 of the umbrella effect.)

Marcela ducked into a small bistro cafe to wait out the storm. The rain is sheeting outside. She ordered a cafezinho, sat at the small dark-wood table by the window. Her primer liso intacto is on the table — she set it down while rifling through her tote for her wallet earlier. Her hair? Still intact from this morning — that's literally the brand's promise being tested in real time. The cafezinho cooled a bit. She picks it up to sip.

**Frame:** Bottle on dark wood beside a small white cafezinho cup with saucer. Rain streams down the window behind, taillights through wet glass. The frame quietly SAYS: this storm cannot reach her hair. 0–2.7s holds (bottle + cup + storm). At 2.7s her cardigan-sleeved hand reaches in from the right, lifts the cup off-frame toward her implied mouth, exits. By 3.5s only the bottle (+ empty saucer) remains. No drinking sounds. The bottle witnesses the storm being held off.

---

## Scene 3 — 6:40 PM. Apartment. (End of day 1, the umbrella held.)

Marcela just got home. She unlocks her door — the hallway light briefly spills into the dim kitchen as she steps through. She closes the door behind her with her hip; the hallway light retracts. She walks to the kitchen island where she always drops her things. Keys land on the counter. Her tote slides off her shoulder. Primer liso intacto is already there — she left it this morning, expecting to maybe refresh before going out tonight (the brand says "reaplique pequenas quantidades nos dias seguintes"). She didn't need to refresh today; the morning application is still holding. Tonight or tomorrow morning, she'll touch up again.

**Frame:** Bottle alone on the warm-gray concrete kitchen island, golden hour through the window. Lighting brightens (hallway light, door opens), returns to baseline (door closes). Soft door click + footsteps. Then her cardigan-sleeved hand reaches in from the right, drops the keys casually on the counter beside the bottle, exits.

---

## Narrative throughline

The product's actual promise — *blindagem contra a umidade por até 24 horas* — IS the structural arc. Morning: she applies it. Midday: the storm attacks (the literal humidity test). Evening: she's home, her hair held all day. The bottle is shown in the frame at every act, the silent constant. The viewer reads the brand promise NOT as a tagline pasted on top but as a story they just watched happen. Marcela never touches the bottle in any of the three shots — she touched it once this morning, off-frame, and her day was held.
