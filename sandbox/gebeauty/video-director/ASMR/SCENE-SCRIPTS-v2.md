# Proof-ad scene scripts — v2 (side-by-side, product-neutral footage)

Supersedes the single-flower sequential structure. Locked with Lucas 2026-06-13.

## Core structure
Every proof ad = **APPLICATION** scene → **EXPOSURE** scene → **CLOSING CARD** (product reveal).
- **Side-by-side**: two objects in frame, **left = untreated, right = treated**. Proof = direct comparison.
- **Two creative versions**: **A = flower** (cream-white peônia) · **B = red silk ribbon** (hair-proxy).
- **Product-neutral footage**: the bottle is NEVER shown in application or exposure. Only the **cap tip** appears, and only in the application START frame. Product identity (cachos coral / liso cream + name) is revealed ONLY on the closing card. → same footage, two end cards = the cachos-vs-liso sales A/B.
- **Aggressor → product (by styling logic, for which end card):** diffuser nozzle → cachos · concentrator nozzle + flat-iron → liso · lighter = dramatic wildcard (either).
- Hand = Marcela canonical right (gJCbKReSXO): coral-red french-tip, twisted gold middle-finger ring.

## SCENE 1 — APPLICATION (shared per version, reused across all exposures)
3 frames:
- **START**: cap-tip ONLY (slim white sprayer, bottle body out of frame) enters diagonally from far right, fingers pump it, a bead of opaque cream-beige gel-creme at the nozzle, aimed at the RIGHT object.
- **MID**: cap-tip has LEFT the scene; a fingertip works the product onto the RIGHT object only.
- **END**: cap gone, hand withdrawn; both objects sit idle (right now carries a faint satin sheen).

## SCENE 2 — EXPOSURE (left untreated fails / right treated survives)
Each exposure = START (both intact + aggressor entering) → END (left damaged, right intact).

### 2.1 Heat — Lighter  (wildcard)
- A flower: left petal tips ignite, blacken, curl, shrivel + smoke; right stays fresh.
- B ribbon: left tip catches, silk shrinks/melts, frays + blackens curling upward; right stays smooth.

### 2.2 Heat — Hair dryer, directional concentrator nozzle  (→ liso)
- A flower: left petals blast back, dry, edges curl/brown, scatter+wilt; right flutters but holds form.
- B ribbon: left whips chaotically, dries, stiffens, frays; right streams smooth, sleek, glossy.

### 2.3 Heat — Hair dryer, diffusion nozzle  (→ cachos)
- A flower: left frizzes — petals lift erratically, dry, kink, wilt; right stays defined + bouncy.
- B ribbon: left puffs, frizzes, kinks; right keeps a smooth defined wave.
