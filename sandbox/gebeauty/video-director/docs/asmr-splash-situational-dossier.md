# Underwater Ingredient-Splash ASMR — Situational-Depth Physics Dossier

**Creative:** 3-beat underwater ingredient-splash ASMR for GE Beauty. Invisible scent (melon / peônia / white musk) rendered as its ingredient sources in art-directed water, resolving to a haircare bottle hero.
**Reused by two variants:** *primer cachos definidos* (warm yellow-sun grade, livelier/bouncier splash) and *primer liso intacto* (cool teal grade, sleek vertical glide). This dossier is the shared physics layer; the grade and motion-energy notes flag where the two variants diverge.

## Beat 1 — MELON

### 1. The instant (6s)
A single fresh melon slice (pale-green honeydew / cantaloupe flesh, no rind clutter) plunges from above the waterline into clear water and decelerates as drag takes over.
- **1s before:** slice is just above a calm water surface, beginning its fall; surface still glassy and unbroken.
- **In-beat:** slice pierces the surface, drags a sheath of air down with it, a burst of bubbles erupts off its wet edges, the slice slows and begins a gentle rocking sink, water visibly displaces around it.
- **1s after:** the eruption settles into a thinning stream of fine bubbles rising off the flesh; slice hangs in slow descent; water clarity returns.

### 2. Underwater physics that MUST be right
- **Entry plume:** a fast object piercing a flat surface drags an air cavity down with it (air entrainment), which collapses into a chaotic burst of mixed-size bubbles, NOT a uniform fizz.
- **Bubble size distribution:** polydisperse — a few large (8-15mm) wobbling, deforming bubbles plus a dense cloud of small (1-3mm) near-spherical ones. Large bubbles wobble and rise fast; tiny ones rise slowly and almost drift.
- **Rise speed:** bubbles accelerate upward then reach terminal velocity; large bubbles clearly outrun small ones. Rise is vertical, allowing for slight lateral wander from turbulence — never falling, never sideways-streaking en masse.
- **Clinging:** micro-bubbles cling to the wet melon flesh and the porous cut surface, releasing in slow strings (nucleation off texture).
- **Buoyancy:** melon flesh is near-neutral to slightly negative — it sinks slowly and rocks, it does NOT plummet like a stone or bob like cork.
- **Clarity + caustics:** water is gin-clear; caustic light-nets ripple across the slice and the bubbles from the disturbed surface above.
- **Refraction:** the slice edge and the bubbles bend/magnify the background slightly; the waterline (if in frame) shows a refracted, wobbling horizon.
- **Suspended particulate:** near zero — a few faint motes catching light for depth, never murk.
- **Waterline:** if visible, it is a living, broken meniscus during entry that re-settles to glass; the splash crown is modest and clean, not a pool-cannonball.

### 3. Must-have visual elements
1. Air-entrainment burst at the moment of entry (the signature "plunge").
2. Polydisperse bubble cloud with visible large-vs-small rise-speed separation.
3. Caustic light-nets rippling over the melon and bubbles.
4. Micro-bubbles clinging to and stringing off the cut flesh.
5. Gin-clear water with faint depth-motes only.

### 4. Must-NOT-have elements
1. Tinted / green / colored water (the melon does NOT dye the water).
2. Murky, cloudy, or turbid water; visible sediment clouds.
3. Cartoon perfectly-round identical bubbles, or a flat carbonated-soda fizz.
4. Ice cubes, frost, condensation, fish, plants, sea life.
5. Soap suds, foam mat, or a frothy surface layer (reads as cleanser, off-brand).

### 5. Light
- **Direction:** strong single key from above-and-slightly-front, mimicking sun through a surface; soft fill so shadows stay open.
- **Color temp:** neutral-to-warm daylight (~5200-5600K). *Cachos variant:* push warm, golden caustics. *Liso variant:* hold neutral, lean cool.
- **On bubbles:** each bubble gets a bright specular pip and a refracted bright rim; backlight makes the bubble cloud glow.
- **On object:** caustics dance across the translucent melon flesh; rim-light catches the wet edge; subsurface glow through the thin slice.

### 6. Audio (ASMR)
- **Present:** the crisp *thip/plunk* of the slice piercing surface; the effervescent crackle-rush of the bubble burst; a soft sustained underwater room-tone (low, pressured hush); fine tinkling of micro-bubbles rising.
- **Absent (meaningful):** no music swell on entry, no whoosh/cinematic boom, no human breath, no ocean-wave ambience. Silence around the crackle makes it premium, not loud.

### 7. Generative-model failure modes → guard clauses
1. **Soda-fizz / carbonation look** (uniform tiny bubbles). → *"polydisperse air bubbles, mix of a few large wobbling 10mm bubbles and many small 1-3mm bubbles, irregular natural distribution, not carbonated, not uniform fizz"*
2. **Tinted green water** (model dyes water to match melon). → *"perfectly clear colorless water, water stays transparent and uncolored, no green tint, no juice dispersing"*
3. **Melon plummets or freezes mid-air** (bad buoyancy / floaty hang). → *"melon slice sinking slowly with gentle rocking, near-neutral buoyancy, slow continuous descent, not falling like a stone, not floating motionless"*
4. **Cannonball splash crown** (pool-jump aesthetic, kitsch). → *"small clean entry, subtle broken surface, restrained splash, no large splash crown, no dramatic water explosion"*
5. **Bubbles fall or drift sideways** (wrong gravity). → *"air bubbles rise upward to the surface, vertical rise, never falling, never sinking bubbles"*
6. **Foam / suds at surface** (reads as soap). → *"no foam, no soap suds, no frothy layer, clean water surface"*

## Beat 2 — PEÔNIA (peony)

### 1. The instant (6s)
Cream-white peony blooms and loose petals drift in the same water; a bloom slowly unfurls / rotates as petals separate and waft.
- **1s before:** a closed-to-half-open peony hovers, just settling from its own gentle entry; water nearly still.
- **In-beat:** petals unfurl and peel away one by one, drifting on micro-currents; the bloom turns slowly; a few petals descend with a leaf-like flutter (slow, swaying fall).
- **1s after:** petals continue their unhurried drift; the densest part of the bloom settles lower while the lightest petals hang suspended; water hush returns.

### 2. Underwater physics that MUST be right
- **Petal fall behavior:** flat, light petals do NOT sink straight — they tumble and sway (flutter/glide), penduluming side to side as they descend, like a leaf in air but slowed ~10x by water's density.
- **Buoyancy:** fresh petals are near-neutral; outer petals hang almost suspended, the dense bloom core sinks slowly. Nothing drops fast.
- **Drag dominance:** motion is heavily damped — every movement is slow, smooth, continuous; no snappy or jittery motion.
- **Bubble role (reduced):** far fewer bubbles than Beat 1 — only a faint sparse stream of micro-bubbles released from petal surfaces and crevices; this is the floral-hush beat, bubbles recede.
- **Water clarity + caustics:** gin-clear; soft caustic nets drift slowly over the cream petals; petals catch subsurface glow.
- **Refraction:** translucent petal edges refract and slightly diffuse light; overlapping petals show soft layered translucency.
- **Suspended particulate:** near zero; maybe the faintest pollen-mote sparkle for life, never debris.
- **Waterline:** ideally not in frame, or a calm distant glassy ceiling; this beat is interior and still.

### 3. Must-have visual elements
1. Petals fluttering/swaying as they fall (leaf-in-water glide, not straight sink).
2. Cream-white petals with translucent, subsurface-glowing edges.
3. Slow bloom rotation / unfurl as the focal motion.
4. Drifting layered petals at varied depths (foreground suspended, background settling).
5. Soft slow caustics over the petals.

### 4. Must-NOT-have elements
1. Petals sinking straight down like stones (no flutter).
2. Colored / pink / saturated flowers or tinted water (keep cream-white, clean).
3. Wilted, browned, bruised, or torn petals (must read fresh, premium).
4. Heavy bubble eruption (that belongs to Beat 1; here bubbles are a whisper).
5. Full stems, leaves, soil, thorns, or a bouquet clutter (single bloom + loose petals only).

### 5. Light
- **Direction:** soft top key, broader and more diffuse than Beat 1; gentle gradient falloff into deeper water.
- **Color temp:** clean neutral with a cream-warm bias on the petals. *Cachos:* warmer cream glow. *Liso:* cooler porcelain white.
- **On petals:** translucent backlight glow through the petal tissue; soft specular sheen on wet surfaces; edges luminous.
- **On bubbles:** the few present bubbles get delicate specular pips, kept subtle.

### 6. Audio (ASMR)
- **Present:** an almost-silent floral hush; the softest velvety brush of petals through water; an occasional single delicate micro-bubble tick; deep calm underwater room-tone.
- **Absent (meaningful):** no bubble crackle-rush (that was Beat 1), no music, no breath, no rustle of dry leaves (it is underwater — sound is damped and round, never crisp/dry).

### 7. Generative-model failure modes → guard clauses
1. **Petals sink straight / lifelessly** (no flutter physics). → *"petals drift and sway as they fall, fluttering and tumbling slowly side to side, leaf-like glide through water, never sinking straight down"*
2. **Pink/saturated peony or tinted water** (model defaults to showy flowers). → *"cream-white peony, soft ivory petals, no pink, no saturated color, water stays perfectly clear and colorless"*
3. **Wilted / bruised / torn petals** (model ages flowers underwater). → *"fresh pristine petals, crisp and unblemished, no wilting, no brown edges, no tears"*
4. **Bubble eruption bleed-over from Beat 1.** → *"minimal bubbles, only a faint sparse stream of tiny bubbles, calm still water, no bubble burst, no eruption"*
5. **Bouquet / stem / foliage clutter.** → *"single peony bloom with loose drifting petals only, no stems, no green leaves, no foliage, clean composition"*
6. **Snappy / fast / jittery motion** (model under-damps the water). → *"slow heavily-damped motion, smooth continuous slow drift, dreamlike slow-motion, water resistance evident"*

## Beat 3 — WHITE MUSK + PRODUCT REVEAL

### 1. The instant (6s)
The haircare bottle (the hero) descends into the same water; melon and petal motifs orbit it; fine air bubbles stream off its matte surface as it settles upright into hero position.
- **1s before:** bottle just enters from above, a soft entry plume trailing it; ingredients beginning to gather.
- **In-beat:** bottle sinks into frame and rotates gently to label-front; a sheath of fine bubbles peels off its matte surface; melon facet + peony petals orbit slowly around it; clean-warmth gather.
- **1s after:** bottle settles upright, near-still, hero-centered and label-readable; a last few bubbles rise off the cap; water calms to glass.

### 2. Underwater physics that MUST be right
- **Bottle entry + air sheath:** a dry-then-submerged matte surface releases a uniform veil of fine micro-bubbles as it wets out (air trapped on the surface texture peeling away), plus a small entry plume. The matte (not glossy) surface holds more clinging micro-bubbles — use this.
- **Buoyancy:** the bottle must read as a controlled descent to a settle, NOT bob to the surface and NOT slam to the bottom. Treat as near-neutral, gently negative — sinks slowly, rights itself, holds.
- **Bubble behavior on hero:** fine bubbles cling to the matte body and cap, release in slow vertical strings off high points and edges; classic "freshly submerged object" sparkle.
- **Orbit motion:** ingredients (one melon facet, a few petals) drift in a slow loose orbit driven by the bottle's wake — they do NOT lock into a rigid ring or mechanical rotation.
- **Clarity + caustics:** gin-clear; caustics ripple across the bottle face and label; the label stays legible (refraction must not warp text into mush).
- **Refraction:** water in front of the label slightly magnifies and ripples but text remains readable; bottle edges refract the background.
- **Suspended particulate:** near zero; depth-motes only.
- **Waterline:** optional soft glassy ceiling above for a sense of "submerged in clean water," with caustics raining down from it.

### 3. Must-have visual elements
1. Veil of fine micro-bubbles peeling off and clinging to the matte bottle as it wets out.
2. Bottle settling upright, label-front, legible, hero-centered.
3. Slow loose orbit of melon facet + peony petals around the bottle (callback to Beats 1-2).
4. Caustic light raking across the bottle and label.
5. Clean clear water resolving to glassy calm as the bottle settles.

### 4. Must-NOT-have elements
1. Bottle bobbing to the surface or slamming to the bottom (wrong buoyancy).
2. Warped / illegible / hallucinated label text or wrong product silhouette.
3. Tinted or murky water; colored "scent clouds" or ink dispersal around the bottle.
4. Glossy plastic over-reflection that kills the matte premium read; cartoon shine.
5. A dry/un-wet bottle pasted into water (no surface wetting, no clinging bubbles) — the cardinal "fake composite" tell.

### 5. Light
- **Direction:** key from above-front to model the bottle form and light the label; gentle rim from behind to separate it from the deep background and to glow the bubble veil.
- **Color temp:** clean warm-neutral (white-musk warmth). *Cachos:* warm golden caustics, sunlit. *Liso:* cool teal grade, sleeker, more vertical light.
- **On bubbles:** rim/backlight makes the clinging bubble veil sparkle and read as 3D around the form.
- **On object:** soft specular roll across the matte body (broad, not hard); caustics animate the label; subsurface-free, clean surface read.

### 6. Audio (ASMR)
- **Present:** a soft *whoom*-then-settle of the bottle displacing water; the fine effervescent shimmer of the bubble veil releasing; a gentle low resolve in the underwater room-tone; a final soft single bubble tick as it settles.
- **Absent (meaningful):** no hard impact thunk, no music sting on reveal, no human voice/VO over the beat (let the product land in hush), no cap-click or mechanical sound.

### 7. Generative-model failure modes → guard clauses
1. **Dry-bottle composite** (no wetting, no clinging bubbles — fake). → *"bottle fully submerged and wetted, fine air bubbles clinging to its matte surface and streaming off edges, surface clearly wet underwater"*
2. **Label warps / text hallucinates.** → *"product label stays sharp, flat, and legible, text undistorted, no warping, accurate product shape"* (and supply the real bottle as reference still).
3. **Bottle floats up or sinks to bottom** (buoyancy break). → *"bottle descending slowly and settling upright, near-neutral controlled sink, holds position centered, not floating to surface, not dropping to floor"*
4. **Colored scent-cloud / ink dispersal around bottle.** → *"clear colorless water, no ink, no colored dispersal, no smoke, no tinted clouds around the bottle"*
5. **Glossy plastic over-shine** (kills matte premium). → *"soft matte bottle surface, broad gentle specular, no hard glossy reflection, no cartoon shine"*
6. **Rigid mechanical orbit of ingredients** (CGI turntable look). → *"melon piece and petals drifting in a slow loose natural orbit on the water current, irregular organic motion, not a rigid ring, not mechanical rotation"*

## Cross-beat continuity

- **Tank / environment:** one continuous body of gin-clear water, same depth and same dark-to-neutral graded background across all three beats. No cut to a different vessel, no surface in one beat and seabed in the next. Read as a single column of clean water the camera lives inside.
- **Bubble style:** identical bubble vocabulary throughout — polydisperse, specular-pipped, vertically rising, clinging-then-releasing off textured surfaces. The *quantity* arcs across beats (Beat 1 erupts → Beat 2 whispers → Beat 3 resolves to a clinging veil), but the *look* of a single bubble never changes.
- **Lighting:** same key direction (above-front), same caustic signature raining from the surface, consistent color-temp spine per variant (warm-golden for *cachos*, cool-teal for *liso*) held across all three beats — only intensity/diffusion shifts (Beat 1 crisp, Beat 2 soft, Beat 3 modeled-for-hero).
- **Particulate + clarity:** same near-zero motes, same gin-clarity in every beat — water is never murkier in one beat than another.
- **Motion-energy spine (variant-level):** *cachos* runs livelier/bouncier (more spring in the splash, more orbit liveliness, warmer); *liso* runs sleeker/vertical (calmer glide, cooler, more controlled descent). Keep this energy consistent within a variant across all three beats so the cut feels like one world, one mood.
