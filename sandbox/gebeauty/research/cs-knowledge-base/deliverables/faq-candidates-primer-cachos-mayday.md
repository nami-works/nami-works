## Source split (important)
- **Loox = per-SKU engine.** Product experience, objections, usage — where the per-product signal lives.
- **CS archive = brand-level engine.** Shipping, returns, payment/antifraude, and policy (cruelty-free / testes em animais). CS is thin on per-SKU product questions — customers rarely name a specific SKU in a question ticket; searches mostly return GE's own agent replies. Do NOT try to force per-SKU FAQ out of CS.

## Handling rule
Objections (scent, "didn't work for me", yield, pump) are the highest-value FAQ items — answer them **honestly** to set expectations. This cuts returns AND negative reviews. Never spin an objection into a fake positive.

## Primer Cachos Definidos (GEB 101)
Signal from 40 reviews.

1. **Deixa o cabelo duro ou com aspecto de gel/molhado?** — HIGH. Dominant reassurance ("não fica duro", "sem aspecto de gel", "sem pesar"). Honest caveat: a minority find it heavy/"pegajoso", usually fine hair using too much.
2. **Como usar? Sozinho, misturado, amassando?** — HIGH. Works alone as a creme, mixed with Booster Definição or leave-in, scrunched on damp hair. Several experiment before finding their way.
3. **Rende pouco? Quanto devo usar?** — RECURRING. "Não dura um mês"; coarse/dense hair needs more, fine hair less.
4. **Serve pro meu tipo de cabelo?** — HIGH. Ondulado, cacheado, 2B, transição capilar, fino e grosso; a few "não funcionou".
5. **O cheiro é forte / parece vencido?** — RECURRING MINORITY (⚠️ care). Real natural fragrance; a few perceive "óleo velho/vencido". Address honestly, no spoilage.
6. **A definição dura? Day-after?** — MEDIUM. "Define por horas", "segura no dia seguinte".
7. **O pump/bico é difícil de usar?** — MEDIUM. Older pump was stiff; reviews confirm it was **improved** — reflect that.

## Máscara Mayday (GEB 121)
Signal from reviews (thinner base than Primer).

1. **Qual a textura? Deixa o cabelo pesado/rígido?** — HIGH. Mousse texture, "não deixa rígido", encorpado.
2. **Posso usar em cabelo com química / descolorido / colorido?** — HIGH & high-value. Used around bleaching/coloring, before and after chemical processes.
3. **O cheiro fixa / é forte?** — MEDIUM. Loved, but "fixou pouco" for some.
4. **Que resultado tenho e em quanto tempo?** — HIGH. Reconstrução, brilho, maciez, "já no primeiro uso".
5. **Como e com que frequência usar?** — MEDIUM. Reconstruction-mask ritual.
