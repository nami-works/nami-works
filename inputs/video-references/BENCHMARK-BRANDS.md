# Benchmark brands for `/video-director` IP

**Creative architecture:** **hook (drive desire/attention) → body (sensorial benefit) → offer (CTA)**.

**Locked filters:**
- Intentionally faceless (product is the star).
- Hero ritual (the regimen) — multi-product or sequence-based.
- Build-up phase aspiring to Crown-Affair/Aesop tier of craft.
- **Category-agnostic.** Hair is the home turf but any category that masters faceless hook→offer is fair game.

**Anti-references locked:**
- Drugstore DR (loud, claims, before/after).
- Influencer-stuffed UGC.
- Beige clean-girl saturation.
- Hyper-clinical is OK if done with taste.

## How to use this list

1. Visit `facebook.com/ads/library` → search the brand → filter to active video ads.
2. Pull the ads that match faceless hook→offer structure (skip face-heavy ones from the same brand).
3. Save as `ref-<NN>-<brand>-<short-slug>.mp4` in this folder.
4. Add a row to [INTAKE.md](INTAKE.md) with lane + one-liner on why it works.

Target depth: **2 to 3 from A**, **1 to 2 from B**, **2 from C**, **1 to 2 from D**, **2 to 3 from E**, **1 to 2 from F**. About 10 to 14 MP4s. Quality > volume.

## A. Hair-specific peers (the home category)

Direct competitive set + craft anchors for hair-as-hero shots.

| Brand | Origin | Why it's a benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| **Crown Affair** | US | THE anchor. Hair-only, faceless, ritual-led. Slow-mo hair-fall, oil-pour macros, warm color grade. |
| **Davines** | Italy | Sustainability + craft. Faceless product photography, botanical context shots, strong art direction across full range. |
| **Sachajuan** | Sweden | Scandi-minimal. Stark sets, color-block product photography, almost zero text. Industrial-luxe counterpoint. |
| **Christophe Robin** | France | French luxury hair. Painterly mask/oil shots, rose-and-cream palette. |
| **Oribe** | US | Premium hair, editorial-level product still life in motion. Production-value bar. |

## B. Faceless beauty peers across other categories

Non-hair beauty brands that nail faceless ritual. Borrow their hook craft, swap the product.

| Brand | Category | Why it's a benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| **Necessaire** | Body | Body-care minimalism with hero-ritual framing. Architectural product photography, faceless, sage/cream palette. Closest cross-category template. |
| **Augustinus Bader** | Skincare | Clinical-luxury without lab-coat cliche. Faceless. Texture-and-light only. The "clinical done with taste" benchmark. |
| **Hanni** | Body | Painterly body care, small brand looking premium. High-taste hook craft on tiny budgets. |
| **Rhode (product-macro ads only)** | Skincare/lip | Skip Hailey face shots, pull the peptide-lip texture macros. Glazed-skin visual language at its peak. |
| **Glossier (product-macro ads only)** | Skincare/color | Same caveat — pull only their "Body Hero", "Universal Pro-Retinol" macro ads, not the face-led ones. |

## C. Sensorial canon (visual grammar masters)

Wellness-adjacent fragrance/skin houses. Use these to teach the *shot vocabulary*. Almost every motion designer in beauty references this set.

| Brand | Why it's a benchmark |
|---|---|
| **Aesop** | THE reference for product-as-poetry. Hand-pour, fabric drop, light through amber glass. Faceless. Watch hair line (Equal, Calm, Classic) specifically. |
| **Le Labo** | Raw industrial luxe. Concrete + brass + linen. Master of product-in-context shots. |
| **Diptyque** | Heritage minimalism. Wax pour and candle flame = masterclass in slow sensorial. |
| **Byredo** | Cold-minimal luxury. Counterpoint to warm palettes. Almost monochrome. |
| **Maude** | Sexual wellness, design-led. Bravest faceless product framing in beauty. |

## D. Brazil + LATAM

Local market peers. Casting (whose hair shown in macro), language register, what "premium" reads as locally.

| Brand | Category | Why it's a benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| **Sallve** | Beauty broad | Brazilian Glossier-equivalent. Watch PT-BR voice without losing premium feel. |
| **B.O.B (Bars Over Bottles)** | Hair | BR hair, sustainability angle, faceless leaning. Closest local hair peer in positioning. |
| **Truss** | Hair pro | Premium pro-hair. Strong still photography, salon-credentialed without humans. |
| **Costa Brazil** | Body/oils | Faceless, ingredient-storytelling, cross-cultural premium model. Direct study for ingredient narrative even in ritual mode. |
| **Simple Organic** | Clean beauty | BR clean beauty. Botanical-led, native sustainability story. |
| **Granado** | Heritage repositioned | BR heritage brand modernized. Useful "old-brand-new-language" pattern. |

## E. Hook craft cross-category — food, bev, home, wellness

These brands solve THE SAME problem GE Beauty solves: drive desire for an inanimate object without a human face. Different SKU, identical hook constraints. Often the best DR craft on Meta sits here.

You previously deprioritized non-beauty references. Including a curated few because **hook→offer structure for faceless product-led DR is more evolved here than in beauty**.

| Brand | Category | Why it's a benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| **Graza** | Olive oil | Master of olive-oil pour hooks. 1.5-second sensorial hook → "use it on bread" → buy. Pure faceless product-as-hero DR. |
| **Brightland** | Olive oil | More minimal than Graza. Painterly pour shots, editorial art direction, faceless. |
| **Fly By Jing** | Sauce | Sizzle macros and pour shots that drive immediate appetite. Genius at the 0-2s hook. |
| **Ghia** | Non-alc apéritif | Dreamy sensorial sound-on hooks. The "I want this in my hand" feeling. |
| **Recess** | CBD bev | Pastel surreal hook craft. Faceless, lifestyle without humans. |
| **Our Place** | Cookware | Sensorial steam, food in pans, sound design. Cross-application of "ritual with the object". |
| **Liquid Death** | Water | Sharp hook craft and anti-establishment voice. Pull as a *voice study*, not a visual one. |

## F. Anti-references — study these to know what to avoid

These exist in the locked anti-pattern set. Pull 1 to 2 ads from each for explicit "don't do this" in the IP doc.

| Brand | Anti-pattern | What we learn |
|---|---|---|
| **Skala / Salon Line / OGX** | Drugstore DR | Loud claims, percentage callouts, before-after splits. Cheap-feeling even when production is fine. |
| **Olaplex (some social ads)** | Influencer-stuffed UGC | Rotating creators saying "I love this". Contradicts faceless. |
| **TikTok-native clean-girl brands** | Beige saturation | Cream-on-vanilla-on-beige with the 2024 wallpaper aesthetic. Already overdone. |
