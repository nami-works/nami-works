Local Delivery — Order details modal

Replaces the current "click order → open in Shopify admin (new tab)" behavior with an in-page Polaris <s-modal> tailored for the LD operator. Surfaces the fields that drive routing/dispatch decisions, defers the long Shopify metafield wall to the "Open in Shopify" deep-link in the footer.

Design rationale

  • Operator-focused, not Shopify-replica. An LD operator needs items, customer contact, address quality, tags, and the recent timeline — not the 22 Iglu/NFC-e metafields. Those stay one click away via the footer deep-link.
  • Items lead. The modal exists primarily to confirm information and make manual adjustments, so the line items get the full-width top slot. The two-column row beneath answers the operator's follow-up questions: "who do I call if it fails?" (Customer, left) and "where do I deliver this?" (Shipping address, right).
  • Items + Totals collapsed into one card — line items typically fit in 1-3 rows for LD orders.
  • Tags editable inline (Hybrid). Top row: clickable quick-toggles for the three operator-relevant tags (ld_failed-delivery, ld_address-confirm, ld_number-confirm) — outlined when absent, filled-warning when present, click to toggle. Below: every other tag as a chip with × to remove, plus a free-text input with autocomplete from the shop's known tag list. Saves immediately on toggle/add/remove (optimistic UI; revert on error). Operator quick-toggles drive auto-assign skip logic so they get the prominent treatment.
  • Internal notes inline-editable. One of the few places the operator can leave a note about a customer interaction without leaving the page.
  • Timeline last 5 events only. Older events available via "Open in Shopify".
  • Footer left = destructive / utility: Open in Shopify is a utility escape hatch. Right = primary action (Close).
