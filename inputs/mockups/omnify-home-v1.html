Operate your omnichannel business on Shopify

Omnify is the Shopify-embedded app for CPG brands selling online and through physical stores. Plan, optimize and dispatch deliveries from retail locations, define monthly goals and track how every store performs, and find your next store location based on where YOUR CUSTOMERS actually live.

All in your Shopify admin.

How it works
Today · Local delivery

Local delivery, from your stores.

Route planning, driver dispatch, order status in one view.

Omnify reads orders tagged for local delivery and clusters them into optimized routes around your store locations. Dispatch to your own drivers or hand off to a connected carrier like Lalamove, without leaving the Shopify admin. Status flows back into the order, the tags, and the customer notification.

See it in the screencast →
This month · Retail sales

Retail sales against goals.

Monthly targets, live pacing, per-location accountability.

Set revenue goals per location and per month. As POS orders come in, the dashboard fills with actual sales against target. Managers see their store's number, headquarters sees the full network, and the gap between goal and actual stays visible all month.

See it in the screencast →
Next year · Footprint expansion

Footprint expansion, on evidence.

Where to open next, based on actual demand.

Omnify combines your order and customer data with map-based analytics to rank cities and neighborhoods where demand is dense enough to justify a new physical location. Save expansion projects, layer them on the map, compare candidate addresses side by side, and export the shortlist.

omnify-home-v1 — design synthesis

  • Hero — Growth copywriter's recommended headline. Holo on "running omnichannel" (animated, white-space: nowrap to stay on a single row). Sub-copy synthesized from PM + Growth, names the 3 capabilities without rigid listing. CTA primary: "See Omnify in action" → /screencast (safer than an un-live App Store listing). CTA secondary: "See features" (same-page anchor, reviewer-friendly). Hero visual: Omnify tree logo at 360px desktop / 280px mobile, same glow + float treatment as cpg-labs.io hero.
  • Feature section — Stacked alternating rows. Per-feature holographic glow color on the image halo: delivery-guy = holo-1 cyan, rocket = holo-3 magenta, map-pin = holo-2 lavender. Image always above copy on mobile (visual hook first). Titles stay solid var(--text) — gradient restraint matches cpglabs-corporate (one animated gradient per screen ceiling).
  • Feature order — PM's time-horizon arc: today (delivery) → this month (retail sales) → next year (footprint). Each feature's eyebrow carries the time label explicitly to make the arc visible. Narrative chain: "we delivered it" → "did we hit goal?" → "where do we grow next?"
  • Copy tightening — PM flagged "in real time" as latency-claim risky. Swapped for "as POS orders come in" per their note. No superlatives anywhere.
  • Scroll-reveal — feature rows fade up on IntersectionObserver. Disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Image float desync — each feature image uses heroFloat with animation-delay 0 / -2s / -4s so the three don't bob in lockstep.
  • Proof band — single-sentence transition before the CTA, with a quiet backlink to cpg-labs.io. Reconnects Omnify to the parent brand for reviewers without being heavy-handed.
  • Final CTA — holographic gradient button ("Watch the demo"), same visual treatment as the Storefront waitlist CTA on cpg-labs.io. Signals "obvious action".
