What we build

Operational software for Shopify merchants.

CPG Labs is an independent software company in São Paulo, Brazil. We build Omnify, a Shopify-embedded app covering route planning, retail analytics, delivery dispatch, and footprint planning. One product, maintained by the team that ships it. Hosted on AWS with encrypted storage, logging, and documented data-handling aligned with GDPR, LGPD, and CCPA. Available to merchants worldwide through the Shopify App Store.
