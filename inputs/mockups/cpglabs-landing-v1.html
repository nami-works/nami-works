Design language

  • Dark-first, light-mode via prefers-color-scheme. Matches the existing CPG Labs holographic aesthetic and keeps the box logo prominent.
  • Holographic gradient reused from cpglabs-home.module.css (tokens #5ecece → #b09fda → #d4a8d4) on the hero headline word "Omnify" and the nav CTA. Consistent with existing brand.
  • Logo used twice, prominently: at 24px in nav + as the ~280px hero visual. This is the signature asset and should anchor the page identity.
  • No lead form, no Calendly, no cost-stack SVG — this is a trust page, not a sales page. Two CTAs total: "Open Omnify on Shopify" (primary) and "email us" (secondary).
  • One-screen hero on desktop, natural scroll fold at "About" section. Each section below ~500px tall.
  • Mobile: single column, hero stacks visual above copy (or below — see Q2).

Copy choices

  • Headline: "The software company behind Omnify." Direct message-match for a reviewer who just saw "Omnify by CPG Labs" on the App Store.
  • No superlatives ("best", "leading", "#1") and no stats — rule 4.3.3/4.3.4 compliance even on the corporate page since reviewers form trust holistically.
  • No em dashes anywhere (per persistent user feedback). Comma/period constructions only.
  • "Built in São Paulo" — concrete location is a trust signal. "Serving merchants worldwide" softens it from a regional-only shop to a global-intent product.
  • Support vs contact email split — two roles. Flags that support@cpg-labs.io MX forwarding needs to be set up in GoDaddy/Google Workspace before this page goes live, OR we drop that block until it's wired.
