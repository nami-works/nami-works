Omnify Reorg — Settings / Locations Mockup

This page shows the new unified Settings > Locations surface. Two collapsible sections replace the old single "Location settings" card: Delivery details (renamed + made collapsible) and Retail goals (migrated from Sales Goals > Settings). Each card has a drill-chip row (reused from Affiliates' pattern) that opens a grid-pinned drill panel below. Settings tab row gains a rename: Providers → Delivery providers.

Design anchors
  • Collapsible chevron pattern copied from Local Delivery's route manager.
  • Chip-triggered drill pattern adapted from Affiliates KPI cards (scaled to pill size because chips here are tertiary to the form fields above them).
  • Grid-pinned rows: chips stay in place when a drill opens — borrowed directly from Affiliates' .overviewGrid CSS.
  • Mutex state: one drill open per card at a time. Second click on the active chip collapses it.
  • Per-section Save with optional "Unsaved changes" dirty-state hint.
  • Include toggle on header: Retail goals uses the section header as both label and on/off switch.
