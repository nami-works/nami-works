Wireframe — hero + trust-bridge + quiz

Built with /design:user-research, /growth-hacker (lp-audit lens), /design:ux-copy, /design:design-critique. Nothing existing is removed — the quiz, diagnostic, tiers, and confirmation flow are untouched below the fold on both options.

Research finding

Cold IG traffic has just seen their exact pain named in an ad (high recognition) but has never heard of nami.works (zero trust) and there's no real social proof yet to lean on. The gap isn't "do they believe the pain is real" (the poster/text hero already nails that) — it's "what happens if I answer these questions, and what does it cost me." That's a mechanism + commitment gap, not an emotional one.

Critique finding that changed the plan

My first draft put a full text section (headline + body) between hero and quiz, always visible. The critique pass flagged two problems: (1) it duplicates work the hero already does (re-validating the pain is redundant right after a poster that already shows someone exactly like you, mid-crisis), and (2) a static section adds scroll friction for someone who's ready to click immediately. Fix: move the reassurance to the moment it's actually needed — right as the quiz reveals, not before. One line, not a section.

Two options to choose between

  • A · recommended Minimal — a one-line reassurance caption appears above "Pergunta 1 de 4" only once the visitor has already clicked the CTA. Hero stays exactly as clean as it is today. Lowest friction, closest to current build.
  • B · fuller Adds a compact "como funciona" 3-step mini-section (visible on page load, below the hero) before the quiz, closer to what a traditional landing page has. More "substantive," costs more scroll/attention from cold traffic before the quiz.
