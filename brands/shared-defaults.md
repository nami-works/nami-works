# Shared brand defaults

Rules that genuinely hold across every CPG Labs brand. A manifest **inherits these by listing them in `voice.shared_defaults`** — declared, never silently assumed. A brand may override any of them in its own manifest.

## Voice / grammar (cross-brand)

- **No em-dash / travessão anywhere.** (GE Beauty and NAMI converge here — this is intentional, not a copy-paste error.)
- **Idiomatic Portuguese, not calques.** Write PT-BR that a native operator would say, never a literal translation of English structure.
- **No invented numbers.** Never fabricate a metric, price, or proof figure. If a number isn't in the brand's source-of-truth, don't state it.
- **Real subjects only.** Reference only real products/services/cases that exist in the brand's catalog or docs.

## Operating posture (cross-brand)

- **Confirm before writes.** Store mutations, customer-facing sends, publishes, and spend are gated on Lucas's explicit approval.
- **Verify, don't trust.** Prefer independent data over execution reports.
- **Escalate money, product, brand.** Tech and mechanics are silent calls; anything touching money assumptions, positioning, or end-customer experience escalates.

Anything brand-specific (taglines, personas, economic floors, lexicon) does NOT belong here — it lives in the per-brand manifest.
