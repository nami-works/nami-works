# Vendor / SaaS — review playbook

## What this covers
Software/tool subscriptions and their order forms, ToS / Master Subscription Agreements, and Data
Processing Agreements. Usually low individual stakes but they pile up and auto-renew quietly.

## Red-flag checklist (Brazil-tuned)
1. **Term + auto-renewal + cancellation window** — when does it renew, and the last date to give
   notice to stop it. Log the renewal date so it never renews by surprise.
2. **Pricing & overage** — included volume, per-unit overage rate, what triggers overage.
3. **Billing cadence & payment terms** — monthly/quarterly/annual, Net-N.
4. **Currency / FX** — USD-billed vendors expose GE to FX swings.
5. **Brazilian remittance taxes** — for software/SaaS paid abroad, factor IRRF, CIDE,
   PIS/COFINS-importação, ISS, and IOF into the *true* cost (often +40-50% over the sticker).
6. **DPA / LGPD** — international data transfer basis if the vendor processes customer data abroad.
7. **Liability cap & warranty disclaimer.**
8. **Data export / portability** on exit (avoid lock-in).

## Locked lessons
- For foreign SaaS billed in USD, the sticker price is not the cost — add withholding taxes + IOF.
- Always capture the renewal/notice date at signing; that's the term that bites later.

## BR-law anchors
Lei 13.709/2018 (LGPD) · withholding regime for software/SaaS remittances (IRRF/CIDE/PIS-COFINS-imp).
