# Receivables financing — review playbook

## What this covers
Antecipação / desconto de duplicatas, cessão de direitos de crédito (to a FIDC or factoring
company), and any structure where GE sells/discounts its receivables for cash. The single most
important early question: **with recourse (`coobrigação` / `direito de regresso`) or without?**
With recourse, GE keeps the default risk — the cash is a loan in substance, not a true sale.

## Red-flag checklist (Brazil-tuned)
1. **Recourse vs non-recourse** — `cessão com coobrigação` (Art. 296 CC) means GE stays jointly
   liable for principal + interest + penalties. Confirm and price accordingly.
2. **Personal guarantee** — `devedor solidário` / `avalista`, waiver of `benefício de ordem`
   (lets the fund go straight at the guarantor). Who is personally on the hook, and is it justified?
3. **Repurchase (`recompra`) triggers** — push to *origin-of-credit defects only*. Watch for broad
   hypotheses: buyer refusal, sale cancellation, and especially **force majeure / caso fortuito**
   (GE buying back risk that isn't its fault).
4. **Repurchase cure period** — how long after notice to repurchase (contract default often 72h).
5. **Repurchase penalty** — cap it (e.g. 2%) and tie to objective events; reject discretionary
   penalties ("at the fund's sole criterion", up to 10% + more).
6. **Repurchase price formula** — must be an objective formula in the contract, not set at the
   fund's discretion.
7. **Customer notification** — sacados (your B2B buyers) told to pay the fund directly. B2B-relationship
   sensitive; know it's happening.
8. **Branch / filiais liability** — watch unlimited joint liability for all branches + waiver of
   separate legal personality.
9. **Effective cost** — convert the monthly rate to effective annual, add per-item fees, borderô,
   TED, and float days. Compare against alternatives (bank line, intercompany loan).
10. **Mechanics** — irrevocable cession, `título executivo extrajudicial`, indefinite term, LGPD
    (you're sharing customers' personal data with the fund/collection consultancy).

## Locked lessons
- **Diff the CONTRACT, not the term sheet.** A term-sheet movement ("condições ajustadas") is not
  proof a binding clause changed. Always verify against the redlined contract clause text.
- A counterparty saying your points were "adjusted" ≠ your requests granted. Map each ask to clause text.
- A counterparty can deflect contract-level redlines by sending an **earlier-stage mandate/proposal** (with a break
  fee + DD conditions + título executivo) instead of the revised contract. Signing the "De Acordo" commits you to the
  process before your clause protections are negotiated — don't sign expecting redlines are in.
- Identify recourse in the first pass — it flips the entire risk profile and the true cost.
- Always surface the cheaper/lower-risk alternative (e.g. intercompany loan) as the BATNA.

## BR-law anchors
Art. 296 Código Civil (coobrigação/solidariedade) · CPC Art. 784 (título executivo extrajudicial) ·
Lei 13.709/2018 (LGPD) · Resolução CVM 175 + CMN 2.907 (FIDC).
